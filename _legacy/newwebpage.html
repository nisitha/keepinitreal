Welcome to
"Keepin it Real"

Introducing Gary (age 74) and Chris (age 49), a travelling father and son bicycle team. We pedal around town on bicycles to interview folks who share their wisdom, humor, and stories. We believe that everyone is an open book just waiting to be heard.

The premise is simple. Your hosts, Gary and Chris, will interview random folks along the streets, parks, beaches, shops and major attractions of San Diego. We always ask permission first and treat everyone with respect and consideration. We post short videos (about ten minutes each) with new content uploaded weekly. The videos are fun, light-hearted and spontaneous.

We are a non-profit, non-commercial, non-political, grassroots venture.

Be sure to bookmark this website to view fresh, new clips weekly. You can find us on Facebook at: Keepin It Real and also YouTube by searching: keepinitrealsd
